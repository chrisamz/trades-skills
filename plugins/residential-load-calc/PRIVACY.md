# Privacy policy: residential-load-calc

Last updated: 2026-10-05

## Summary

This plugin sends nothing to its author. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no telemetry.

## What the plugin handles

The details of a job you describe: floor area, appliance and equipment ratings, service size and, if you provide it, a peak demand figure from your utility. The calculator reads one job file and prints a worksheet.

## What leaves your computer

- **To the author:** nothing.
- **To anyone else:** nothing. The calculator works offline and makes no network requests.

Your conversation with Claude is handled by Anthropic under Anthropic's own privacy policy. This plugin does not change that.

## Storage and retention

The plugin stores nothing by itself. Job files and worksheets exist only where you or Claude save them on your computer. Because the author receives no data, there is nothing for the author to retain, share or delete.

## Children

The plugin is a tool for electrical planning and is not intended for anyone under 18.

## Changes

If the plugin ever handles data differently, this file will be updated in the same release and the change described in the release notes.
